FAQ

Good questions

How do I order?

Pick your drinks, add them to your cart, then check out with your name, phone number and pickup time. We'll have it ready at the trailer.

When will my order be ready?

Most orders are ready in about 10-15 minutes. Busy times can take longer, so call or text 775-455-7714 if you're in a hurry.

Where do I pick up?

At the Coffee Stains trailer in Battle Mountain, NV. Our spot can change, so check Facebook or Instagram for today's location.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Call or text 775-455-7714 as soon as you can. Once we've started your drink we may not be able to change it.

Do you have sugar free or non dairy options?

Yes. Add it to your order notes at checkout, or ask when you pick up.

How do I pay, and is tax included?

You pay at checkout. Menu prices are before sales tax, which is added to your total. Tips are optional.

What is your refund policy?

If something isn't right with your order, tell us right away and we'll make it right. Orders cancelled before we start them are refunded in full.

Do you offer delivery?

Not at the moment. All orders are for pickup at the trailer.
